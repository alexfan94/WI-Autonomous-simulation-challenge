# EV Simulation Report

## Results

| Gear Ratio | Time to 20 m/s | Top Speed |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 7 | 10.88 s | 56.49 m/s |
| 9 | 8.36 s | 46.55 m/s |
| 10.5 | 7.29 s | 39.90 m/s |

## Gear Ratio Tradeoff

A higher gear ratio provides more torque multiplication at the wheels, so the vehicle accelerates faster. However, it also makes the motor reach its maximum RPM at a lower vehicle speed, which reduces the top speed.

## Comparison with Part 2

The simulation does not exactly agree with the 10 second estimate for a gear ratio of 7. The simulation takes about 10.88 seconds because it includes the 90% drivetrain efficiency, so not all of the motor torque reaches the wheels.
